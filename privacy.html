Privacy Policy

Splash Twist: Color Sort Game (package com.sharkstudio.splashtwist) by Shark Studio. Last updated: 25 November 2026.

This policy explains what the game collects and why. The game is for players aged 13 and older. Contact: aceable1998@gmail.com.

What we collect

  • Device identifier. We use a device identifier to give guest progress back after a reinstall on the same phone.
  • Google sign-in (optional). Only to save your progress and use it on any phone. Your Google email is shown only to you in Settings and is never shown to other players.
  • Game progress (cloud save). Cleared levels, stars, coins, boosters, hearts and daily ad counts are stored on our server (Supabase, hosted in the EU, Frankfurt).
  • Leaderboard. Your player name (for example "Player 1234", your Google first name, or a name you choose), your avatar number and your scores are shown to all players. We show your Google first name on the leaderboard if you sign in with Google. You can change the name in your Profile.
  • Ads. The game may show optional rewarded ads (watch an ad to get coins). Our ad partners may use the advertising ID of your phone to show and measure ads. In the EU you are asked for consent first. You can reset or turn off the advertising ID in your Android settings.
  • Purchases and virtual items. Coins and boosters are virtual items inside the game.

We do not sell your personal data. We do not collect your location, contacts, photos or microphone.

Who can see your data

Your name, avatar and scores are public on the leaderboard. Your progress is private to your account. Our service providers (Supabase for the server, Google for sign-in, and the ad partners) process data for us under their own privacy policies.

How long we keep data

  • Guest accounts that are not used for 1 year are deleted. Empty guest accounts that can no longer be reached are deleted after 30 days.
  • Other accounts stay until you ask us to delete them.

Delete your account and data

Send an email to aceable1998@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my account". Write the player name you see in your Profile (and the Google email if you signed in with Google). We delete your progress, stars, wallet history, device link and leaderboard entry within 30 days. Deleting cannot be undone. This also works if you no longer have the game installed.

Children

The game is not meant for children under 13. If you think a child under 13 has an account, email us and we will delete it.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, or to delete it. Email aceable1998@gmail.com.

Changes

If we change this policy, we change the date at the top.
