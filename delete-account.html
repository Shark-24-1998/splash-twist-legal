Delete your Splash Twist account

App: Splash Twist: Color Sort Game by Shark Studio (com.sharkstudio.splashtwist)

How to request deletion

  1. Send an email to aceable1998@gmail.com with the subject Delete my account.
  2. In the email, write the player name you see in your Profile in the game. If you signed in with Google, also write the Google email you used. If you play as a guest, the player name is enough.
  3. We reply to confirm and delete your account within 30 days.

You do not need the game installed to send this request.

What we delete

  • Your account and login (guest or Google link).
  • Your game progress: cleared levels, stars, coins, boosters, hearts and daily ad counts.
  • Your wallet history and device link.
  • Your player name, avatar and your entry on the leaderboard.

What we keep

We keep nothing about your account after deletion. Backup copies of our server are overwritten within 30 days. Deleting cannot be undone.
